Capture The Flag

Wednesday, September 23, 2026 · Porto, Portugal · That's a wrap

The AppSec Days Portugal CTF ran on the evening of September 23. Challenges spanned a wide range of security domains, from web vulnerabilities and reverse engineering to cryptography and binary exploitation, at every level from first-timer to professional bug hunter.

All skill levels Live, jeopardy-style Evening of September 23

What the challenges were like

Jeopardy-style, and pitched at every level of experience

Beginner Friendly

Introductory challenges were built to be approachable for first-timers, with guidance and community support on site.

Real-World Challenges

Challenges were rooted in real-world security scenarios across a wide range of domains and disciplines.

Hard Mode Available

Seasoned bug hunters found advanced challenges that demanded deep technical knowledge and creative exploitation.
